Note : Steps 1-3 (in Revisions_arbitrary_plevs.txt)  have to be redone each time you want to process a klayers rtp
with different layers than L2100 USStd
ie after each compilation kCARTA can handle two cases only
a) a 100 layer L2100 USStd pressure levels (fromm 1100 mb to 0.005 mb,
   with 0.25 km thick layers at ground
b) a 100 layer abitrary USStd pressure levels (from  <= 1100 mb to >= 0.005 mb)
Examples of the latter include
- OCO2 PBL layering for Bill Irion/JPL (1050 mb to 0.005 mb,
  with 0.125 km thick layers at surface
- NASA ER2 aircraft at 20 km layering (1100 mb to 56 mb)
  with 47m (0.047 km) thick layers at surface and 0.71 km thick layers at 56 mb
- NASA Gulfstream aircraft at 11 km layering (1100 mb to 250 mb)
  with 25m (0.025 km) thick layers at surface and 0.38 km thick layers at 12 km

We also plan to make a few klayers codes for uplooking instruments at the ground (1013 mb, 980mb, 630 mb)

1) Edit/run make_arbitrary_TZ_STDparams_f90.m

  ~/git/kcarta_gen/MATLAB/EARTH_MAKEIR/make_arbitrary_TZ_STDparams_f90.m
and then re-compile KCARTA to pick up these defns

12/17/24        ARBITRARY PLEVS : subr xWeights
                n_rtp.f90 checks to see how "close" the mean prssure levels in 
                   PLEV_KCARTADATABASE_AIRS (from ../INCLUDE/TempF90/KCARTA_databaseparam.f90) 
                and
                   raPressLevels (from p.plevs, from reading in the rtp file)
                are.

                  iaaOverrideDefault(3,8) = +1 means klayers plevs must agree   with included ODdatabase plevs

                   if PLEV_KCARTADATABASE_AIRS and raPressLevels    agree, and iaaOverrideDefault(3,8) = -1 ... yay, can go ahead (the code will interpolate in T)
                      PLEV_KCARTADATABASE_AIRS and raPressLevels disagree, and iaaOverrideDefault(3,8) = -1 ... yay the code will interpolate in T and P

                After this the code automatically calls SUBROUTINE xWeights to figure out how to weight the kCompressed Databse layers

03/18/11        ARBITRARY PLEVS : subr xWeights
                Faster version of v1.15 : the uncompression routines have been 
                rewritten to take advantage of the fact that profile remains
                the same ==> T offset indices remain the same. This duplicates
                the Matlab version, including the fact that we use linear 
                interpolations

                The code further splits into two, depending on iSplineType
                  iSplineType = 1 : kComp Database, klayers pressure levels 
                                    differ, so need two pressure interp points
                                       use four  weights for most gases
                                       use eight weights for water
                  iSplineType = 2 : kComp Database, klayers pressure levels 
                                    same, so need one pressure interp point
                                       use two  weights for most gases
                                       use four weights for water
